# Commercial audit

## Description
A browser city-builder on a chunked voxel engine: an isometric city whose buildings grow automatically from a simulation of resources, population and trade. A playable session guides the player through the first economic catalysts.

## Technical value
A custom voxel engine (sparse storage, greedy meshing in workers, adaptive quality) plus a city simulation with automatic building growth: the largest codebase in the portfolio after Capsar.
